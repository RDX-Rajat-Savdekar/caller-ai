# Frontend design

Read [console.md](console.md) first. The brief there is the current look: **light Uber-style dashboard**. It wins over `docs/build-plan.md` §7.

## Locked direction

White cards on `#f3f3f3`. Plenty of space inside cards. Pill nav and pill buttons. Black primary actions. Green for reached/safe. Unreached stays the loudest metric.

## Still banned

- Inter, Roboto, Arial as the product voice
- Dark near-black dashboards and amber-on-olive duty boards
- Tracked-out ALL-CAPS eyebrows
- Purple or aurora gradients
- Architecture lectures in the UI copy

## After you build

Load the changed routes. Keep budget, kill, masked numbers, and struck-through unsupported fields.
